Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (or an OAuth identity if you sign up with Google or GitHub).

Payment information

When you purchase a course, payment is processed by our payment provider. We store the transaction reference, amount, currency, and status — we do not store your card details.

Contact information

If you submit a project inquiry, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email, company, and project description.

Course progress

If you enroll in a course, we track your lesson completion and progress so you can resume where you left off.

OAuth information

If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from that provider, as permitted by their APIs.

Downloads and waitlists

If you request a free download, such as our Automation Readiness Scorecard or one of our apps, or join a product's waitlist, we collect your name and email address (the name is optional for waitlists), when you signed up, and where you came from, for example an Instagram link or another website, as shown in the link you followed. We also record your IP address to protect the form from abuse and, for downloads, how many times your personal download link was used.

We use your email address to send what you asked for: your download link, or one email when a product you are waiting for launches. Each form also has an optional box, unticked unless you tick it, to receive occasional emails from us about automation and our products. Ticking it is never a condition of the download or the waitlist. If you tick it, we record when you did, and every email we send you contains a link to unsubscribe at any time. We do not sell your details or share them with anyone for their own marketing.

Visits to this site

We count visits ourselves, without cookies and without any third-party analytics service. For each page viewed we record the page, the time, the website or campaign that sent you (for example "instagram"), your type of device (desktop, mobile or tablet), and whether you were signed in.

To count unique visitors without identifying anyone, we combine your IP address and browser details with a secret that changes every day, and keep only the resulting code. We do not store your IP address or browser details for this. The code cannot be turned back into either, and it stops matching you the next day. We use no analytics cookies; the cookies we do use are described in our Cookie Policy.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to operate your account, deliver purchased courses and downloads, respond to inquiries, secure the platform, understand how the site is used through the cookie-free visit counts described above, and, only if you have opted in, send you occasional emails.

Legal bases (for visitors in the UK and EU)

  • To do what you ask us to: creating your account, delivering courses you buy, and sending a download link or launch email you requested.
  • Your consent: occasional emails from us, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email. Withdrawing does not affect emails already sent.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping the site secure and the forms free of abuse, answering inquiries, and counting visits in the cookie-free way described above. You can object to this use.
  • Legal obligations: keeping payment and tax records.

Service Providers

We share data with service providers strictly as needed to operate the platform: our payment processor, our email delivery provider, and our hosting provider.

Embedded Instagram Videos

Our Reels page lets you play our Instagram videos on this site. The player is provided by Instagram (part of Meta Platforms) and loads only when you press play. From then on, Instagram receives information about that visit directly from your browser, such as your IP address, browser details and the video you watched, and may set cookies, as described in the Instagram Privacy Policy and our Cookie Policy.

We do not send Instagram any information about you, and we do not receive any information from Instagram about who watched our videos on this site.

YouTube API Services

{{ site_name }} uses YouTube API Services to publish our own short-form videos to our own YouTube channel through an internal scheduling tool. By using any feature of ours that connects to YouTube, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service , and your information is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy .

When a channel owner authorises the tool, we receive an OAuth access token and refresh token, and read the channel's name to confirm the right channel is connected. We use the token only to upload videos and set their title, description, tags and privacy status. We do not access, store or share viewer data, comments, analytics or any other YouTube user data, and we do not share the token or any YouTube data with third parties. The token is stored only on the device that runs the tool; no cookies are placed in connection with YouTube API Services.

You can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page . To have any stored YouTube API data deleted, contact us at the address below; we will delete it within 7 days of your request.

Data Retention

We retain account and purchase data for as long as your account is active, and as needed to comply with legal and tax obligations.

  • Downloads and waitlists: if you did not opt in to emails, your sign-up is deleted automatically one year after you made it. If you opted in, we keep it until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Once you unsubscribe, your sign-up is kept marked as unsubscribed, so that we do not email you again, until the same one-year rule removes it.
  • Visit records: deleted automatically after 180 days.
  • Download links stop working after 7 days.

Security

We use industry-standard practices including password hashing, encrypted connections, and server-side authorization checks to protect your data.

Your Rights

You may ask us for a copy of your personal data, and to correct it, delete it, restrict how we use it, or give it to you in a portable format. You can object to any use based on our legitimate interests, and withdraw consent for emails at any time with the unsubscribe link in any email from us. Contact us at the address below; we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to a data protection authority: in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), in the EU the authority in your country, or in Nigeria the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

International Transfers

Our website, database and email are run by service providers whose servers may be in a country other than your own. If you are in the UK or EU, this means your data may be processed outside the UK or EU, in countries those regions have not assessed as having equivalent data protection laws. We protect it there as this policy describes, and you can contact us for more information about where it is processed.

Children's Privacy

This platform is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
