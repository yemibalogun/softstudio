Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (or an OAuth identity if you sign up with Google or GitHub).

Payment information

When you purchase a course, payment is processed by our payment provider. We store the transaction reference, amount, currency, and status — we do not store your card details.

Contact information

If you submit a project inquiry, we collect the details you provide, such as your name, email, company, and project description.

Course progress

If you enroll in a course, we track your lesson completion and progress so you can resume where you left off.

OAuth information

If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from that provider, as permitted by their APIs.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies as described in our Cookie Policy . Analytics cookies are only active if you opt in.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to operate your account, deliver purchased courses, respond to inquiries, secure the platform, and — only with consent — understand site usage through analytics.

Service Providers

We share data with service providers strictly as needed to operate the platform: our payment processor, our email delivery provider, and our hosting provider.

Embedded Instagram Videos

Our Reels page lets you play our Instagram videos on this site. The player is provided by Instagram (part of Meta Platforms) and loads only when you press play. From then on, Instagram receives information about that visit directly from your browser, such as your IP address, browser details and the video you watched, and may set cookies, as described in the Instagram Privacy Policy and our Cookie Policy.

We do not send Instagram any information about you, and we do not receive any information from Instagram about who watched our videos on this site.

YouTube API Services

{{ site_name }} uses YouTube API Services to publish our own short-form videos to our own YouTube channel through an internal scheduling tool. By using any feature of ours that connects to YouTube, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service , and your information is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy .

When a channel owner authorises the tool, we receive an OAuth access token and refresh token, and read the channel's name to confirm the right channel is connected. We use the token only to upload videos and set their title, description, tags and privacy status. We do not access, store or share viewer data, comments, analytics or any other YouTube user data, and we do not share the token or any YouTube data with third parties. The token is stored only on the device that runs the tool; no cookies are placed in connection with YouTube API Services.

You can revoke the tool's access at any time from the Google security settings page . To have any stored YouTube API data deleted, contact us at the address below; we will delete it within 7 days of your request.
