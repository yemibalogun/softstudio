JSWM Abbreviator ("the app") is a writing aid from {{ site_name }} for looking up Joint Service Writing Manual abbreviations and abbreviating Word documents. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Android app. For this website, see our Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or usage data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it does not request permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Your Documents

When you choose or share a Word document with the app, it is read on your device only, so the app can list the terms it contains. A temporary working copy is kept in the app's private cache while you review it and is deleted when the app next starts. The abbreviated copy is saved only where you choose with Android's save picker. Your original document is never changed.

Information Kept on Your Device

The app remembers your review choices (which terms you untick and which alternative abbreviations you prefer) and the "States & countries" setting in its private storage on your phone. Android's own device backup may include these preferences if you have backup turned on; documents are never included. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes them.

Clipboard

When you tap a result, its abbreviation is copied to your device's clipboard so you can paste it. The app does not read the clipboard.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
