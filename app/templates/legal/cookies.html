This page explains how {{ site_name }} uses cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that let a website remember information about your visit.

Necessary Cookies

Always enabled. Used for authentication, session management, security (including CSRF protection), and remembering your cookie preferences. The site cannot function without these.

Preference Cookies

Used to remember settings such as your cookie consent choices across visits.

Analytics Cookies

We do not use analytics cookies. We count visits without any cookies, using a code that changes every day and cannot identify you, as explained in our Privacy Policy.

Marketing Cookies

We do not use marketing or advertising cookies, and we do not run advertising trackers on this site.

Third-Party Cookies

Where third-party services (such as a payment provider's checkout page) set their own cookies, those are governed by that provider's own policy.

Instagram videos

Our Reels page shows videos from our Instagram account. Nothing from Instagram loads when you open the page. Only when you press play on a video does Instagram's embedded player load, provided by Instagram (part of Meta Platforms). From that moment Instagram may set its own cookies and receive information such as your IP address, browser details and which video you watched, as it would if you visited Instagram directly.

Those cookies are set by Instagram, not by us, and are governed by the Instagram Cookies Policy. If you would rather Instagram set no cookies, do not press play: the Reels page itself sets none.

Cookie Duration

Necessary cookies typically expire when you close your browser or after your session ends. Your consent preference is stored for up to one year.

Managing Your Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time using the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer. Today this site sets only the necessary and preference cookies described above, so the optional choices in our cookie banner have no effect. If we ever add optional cookies, they will load only after you agree, and we will update this policy first.
