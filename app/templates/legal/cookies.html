This page explains how {{ site_name }} uses cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that let a website remember information about your visit.

Necessary Cookies

Always enabled. Used for authentication, session management, security (including CSRF protection), and remembering your cookie preferences. The site cannot function without these.

Preference Cookies

Used to remember settings such as your cookie consent choices across visits.

Analytics Cookies

Optional. Help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. These are never loaded until you opt in via the cookie banner.

Marketing Cookies

Optional. Used to measure the effectiveness of marketing activity. Like analytics cookies, these load only after consent.

Third-Party Cookies

Where third-party services (such as a payment provider's checkout page) set their own cookies, those are governed by that provider's own policy.

Cookie Duration

Necessary cookies typically expire when you close your browser or after your session ends. Your consent preference is stored for up to one year.

Managing Your Preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time using the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer.
